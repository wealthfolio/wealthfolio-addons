# Official Addons

Official addons are built, distributed, and supported by Wealthfolio. They are
the only addons installable directly from within the app.

| Addon | Description | Status | Version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Goal Progress Tracker | A simple addon to track your investment progress towards target amounts with a visual representation | active | 3.6.2 |
| Investment Fees Tracker | Track and analyze investment fees across your portfolio with detailed analytics and insights | active | 3.6.2 |
| Swingfolio: Simple Stock Trading Tracker | A simple swing stock trading tracker with performance analytics and calendar views | active | 3.6.2 |
